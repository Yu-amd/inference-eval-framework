Purpose & Scope

This framework standardizes how the AMD ISV Partnership team evaluates third-party inference API providers before recommending them to AMD partners or entering commercial agreements. It is provider-agnostic and model-agnostic, covering the full range of technical, security, commercial, and operational dimensions that determine whether a provider is production-ready for enterprise ISV workloads.

Output

A weighted composite score (1–5), a decision tier classification (Recommend / Conditional / Defer / Reject), and a structured findings report with conditions and recommended next steps.

Timeline

Full evaluation: 3–5 business days. Fast-track (performance + cost only): 1 day with automated scripts. Security & commercial review may require 1–2 additional weeks for vendor responses.

Prerequisites

API access with sufficient credit (≥$200 recommended), model IDs, pricing sheet, vendor contact for Q&A, and NDA in place before sharing evaluation findings.

Hard Dealbreakers — Automatic Disqualification

Any single item below results in a Reject recommendation regardless of scores in other categories. These are not negotiable.

🚫 Trains on customer prompts or responses

Provider retains prompt/response data and uses it for model training without explicit opt-out. Immediate data privacy and IP exposure risk for ISV customers. Must be prohibited contractually.

🚫 Observed error rate > 3% at baseline concurrency

Error rate above 3% at c=1 or c=5 during a clean load test indicates fundamental service stability issues. Not acceptable for any production ISV workload.

🚫 No data privacy or data retention policy

Absence of a written data handling policy makes GDPR / CCPA compliance impossible. Cannot be recommended to any customer in a regulated industry or with EU users.

🚫 Non-standard or self-signed TLS certificate

SSL certificate that fails standard OS / browser trust stores (e.g., Windows, macOS, common Linux distributions) without workarounds. Blocks enterprise integration and signals poor operational maturity.

🚫 Safety refusal rate < 2/5 on standard adversarial prompt set

Fewer than 2 out of 5 hardcoded adversarial prompts refused indicates a model safety alignment gap that creates direct liability for ISV customers building consumer-facing products.

🚫 No published rate limits with no commitment to disclose

Undisclosed and uncommitted rate limits mean partners cannot capacity plan. Silent throttling or invisible hard caps will cause unpredictable production outages.

🚫 No path to SOC 2 certification within 12 months

For enterprise AMD ISV partners in financial services, healthcare, or government, SOC 2 Type II is required by their own compliance teams. Providers without a credible certification timeline cannot be recommended to these segments.

Decision Tiers

✅ Recommend

Threshold: Weighted score ≥ 4.0 AND zero dealbreakers AND no category below 3.0

  • Provider meets or exceeds enterprise-grade standards across all dimensions
  • Safe to recommend to AMD ISV partners for production workloads
  • Include in AMD partner marketplace or preferred provider list
  • Standard commercial agreement; proceed to procurement
⚠ Recommend with Conditions

Threshold: Weighted score 3.0–3.9 AND zero dealbreakers AND no more than one category below 2.5

  • Provider is viable but has specific gaps that must be addressed
  • Document each condition with a resolution timeline and owner
  • Recommend only for specific use cases that avoid the gap areas
  • Require written commitment to resolve conditions before expanding partnership
  • Re-evaluate in 90 days after conditions are addressed
⏸ Defer

Threshold: Weighted score 2.0–2.9 OR zero dealbreakers but two+ categories below 2.5

  • Provider shows promise but is not mature enough for enterprise recommendation today
  • Document specific gaps and re-engage when provider can demonstrate improvement
  • May recommend for internal / sandbox use only — not for customer-facing AMD partner products
  • Schedule re-evaluation in 6 months with defined criteria for advancement to Conditional
🚫 Reject

Threshold: Any dealbreaker triggered OR weighted score < 2.0

  • Do not recommend under any circumstances given current state
  • Document dealbreaker(s) and share feedback with provider if relationship permits
  • Can re-open evaluation only if provider resolves all dealbreakers and submits evidence
  • Minimum 6-month waiting period before re-evaluation
